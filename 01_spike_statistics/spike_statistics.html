Exercise 17 Creata a sine wave with a frequency of 0.5 Hz and an amplitude of 5 Hz, that goes from 0 s to 5 s at a sampling rate of 1 kHz. Then, add the amplitude to the sine wave and plot the resulting signal

# How do we reverse the process - estimating the time-varying firing rate from the observed spikes?
t_start = 0 * pq.s
t_stop = 5 * pq.s
frequency = 0.5 * pq.Hz
amplitude = 5 * pq.Hz
sampling_rate = 1000 * pq.Hz
duration = t_stop - t_start
n_samples = int(duration * sampling_rate)
t = np.linspace(t_start, t_stop, n_samples)
signal = amplitude*np.sin(2*np.pi*frequency*t)
signal += amplitude 
plt.plot(t, signal)
